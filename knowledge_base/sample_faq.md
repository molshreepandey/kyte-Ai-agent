# Demo Wallet - Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Demo Wallet?
Demo Wallet is a digital money-management app that lets users send money, pay bills, track spending and save automatically from one place. It is available on Android and iOS.

## How do I create a Demo Wallet account?
Download the Demo Wallet app, enter your mobile number, verify the one-time password (OTP), and complete your KYC by uploading a government ID and taking a selfie. Most accounts are approved within 10 minutes.

## What documents are needed for KYC?
You need one government-issued photo ID (passport, driving licence, or national ID card) and a selfie. Address proof is required only for transfers above 5,000 USD per month.

## Are there any account fees?
The Demo Wallet Basic plan has no monthly fee. Demo Wallet Plus costs 4.99 USD per month and Demo Wallet Premium costs 9.99 USD per month. There are no hidden maintenance charges.

## What are the transfer limits?
Demo Wallet Basic allows up to 1,000 USD per day and 5,000 USD per month. Demo Wallet Plus allows up to 5,000 USD per day and 25,000 USD per month. Demo Wallet Premium allows up to 20,000 USD per day with no monthly cap.

## How fast are transfers?
Transfers between two Demo Wallet users are instant and free. Bank transfers arrive within 1 business day on Basic and within 30 minutes on Plus and Premium. International transfers take 1 to 2 business days.

## What does an international transfer cost?
Demo Wallet charges a flat 1.5% fee on Basic, 0.8% on Plus and 0.4% on Premium. Exchange rates are the mid-market rate with no markup.

## What should I do if my card is lost or stolen?
Open the app, go to Cards, tap the card, and choose Freeze Card. The freeze is instant. Then tap Replace Card to order a new one, which arrives in 5 to 7 business days. You can also call our 24x7 support line.

## How do I reset my Demo Wallet PIN?
Go to Settings > Security > Reset PIN, verify with an OTP sent to your registered mobile, and set a new 4-digit PIN. For safety, you cannot reuse your last three PINs.

## How can I contact support?
In-app chat is available 24x7. Phone support is available from 8 AM to 10 PM IST at 1800-123-3366. Email: support@demowallet.example. Premium users get a dedicated priority line.
