# Demo Wallet - Product Specifications

## Demo Wallet Save (Auto-Savings)
Demo Wallet Save rounds up every purchase to the nearest whole unit and moves the difference into a savings pot. The default interest rate is 5.2% APY on balances up to 10,000 USD and 3.0% APY above that. Withdrawals from savings pots are free and settle instantly. Users can create up to 10 pots with custom goals.

## Demo Wallet Card
The Demo Wallet Card is a virtual and physical debit card on the Visa network. Virtual cards are issued instantly; the physical card is free on Plus and Premium and costs 5 USD on Basic. Cashback is 1% on Basic, 2% on Plus and 3% on Premium for groceries and dining, capped at 50 USD per month. Card spending limits can be changed any time in the app.

## Demo Wallet Bills
Demo Wallet Bills lets users pay electricity, water, internet, mobile and subscription bills. Users can set up auto-pay and get reminders 3 days before the due date. There is no fee for bill payments. Failed auto-pays are retried once after 24 hours.

## Demo Wallet Insights
Demo Wallet Insights automatically categorises all spending into groups such as food, travel, and rent, and shows monthly trend charts. Users can set category budgets and receive an alert at 80% usage. Insights data is computed on-device and never sold to third parties.

## Security
All data is encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Demo Wallet supports biometric login, device binding and real-time fraud alerts. Any suspicious transaction triggers an instant push notification and a one-tap block option.

## Demo Wallet Plans Summary
- Basic: 0 USD/month, 1% cashback, 1.5% international fee.
- Plus: 4.99 USD/month, 2% cashback, 0.8% international fee, faster bank transfers.
- Premium: 9.99 USD/month, 3% cashback, 0.4% international fee, priority support, no monthly transfer cap.
